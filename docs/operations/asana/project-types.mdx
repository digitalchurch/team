# Project Types in Asana

We have several project types in asana. Here's an overview of the types of projects we do.

## Custom Website

This project includes six phases to produce the best product. Here are the phases.

1. Onboarding
2. Discovery
3. Strategy
4. Build
5. Revisions
6. Launch

## Custom Native app

The custom native app project is very similar to the custom website project, but with different deliverables.

## Template Based Website

Our template based website projects are customizations of pre-built websites. This is a quick version of the custom website build. The phases are as follows:

1. Onboarding
2. Setup
3. Revisions
4. Launch

## Unlimited Development

One of our ongoing plans includes unlimited revisions of the website. This can be applied to either a custom built website or a template based website. In these cases, the client is free to email us anytime they want with additional revisions or updates to the website.

## Custom Retainer

For our clients who want the most engagement from our team, we offer custom retainer services. Every client will be different, and we will document how we work with each client to fulfill their individual needs. This can cover any of the following areas of focus:

1. Social Media Marketing
2. Content Writing
3. Design
4. Development
5. General IT needs
6. Search Optimizations
7. Performance Optimizations
