# Social Media Publishing

For The B Brave Foundation, we follow a specific protocol oulined below.

1. Scaffold the Posts in [Craft](https://s.craft.me/MpUioKiwOd9QAV) (Quarterly)   
2. Write the Posts in Craft (Monthly)
3. Design the Images in [Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/NqUoHGznGZw5cMk9MXMmeW/B-Brave-Foundation?node-id=2269-2) (Monthly)
4. Post via [Post-Bridge](https://www.post-bridge.com/) (Weekly)
5. Monitor and adjust as needed.

We follow a posting rhythm that targets a baseline of **two-posts per week**, with additional posts as needed.
